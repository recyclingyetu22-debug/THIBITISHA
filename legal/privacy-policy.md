# THIBITISHA Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 August 2026_

This policy explains what information THIBITISHA ("the app", "we", "us")
collects when you use the THIBITISHA mobile app, and how it's used.

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you register, we collect your name, email
address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in
readable form). We also collect the organization name you register with —
an individual user registering on their own gets a one-person organization
automatically.

**Documents you submit.** When you upload a document for verification, we
store the file itself and the results of our forensic examination (the
findings, assessment, and any notes an investigator records). This is the
core function of the app — without storing the document, we can't show you
its analysis history or let an investigator review it later.

**Usage records.** We keep a record of each verification you submit (when,
from which app version, by which account) so your account's usage and
verification history can be shown back to you, and so your free/paid
verification balance can be tracked accurately.

**Technical information.** Standard technical data your device sends with
any network request (IP address, general request timing) may appear in
server logs, kept only as long as needed for security and debugging.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data to anyone.
- We do not share your documents with third parties for advertising or
  marketing purposes.
- Document analysis is currently performed entirely by our own
  rule-based forensic engine — your documents are not sent to a
  third-party AI service unless we explicitly update this policy to say
  otherwise before doing so.

## Payments

Verification packages purchased through the app are processed through the
Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems. We receive confirmation
that a purchase was made, but your payment card details are handled
entirely by Apple/Google and never reach our servers.

## Document retention & deletion

Documents and their analysis results are kept for as long as your account
is active, so you can refer back to past verifications. You can request
deletion of your account and associated documents at any time by
contacting us (see below) — we will delete them within a reasonable time,
except where we're required to retain records for legal or audit reasons.

## Data isolation

Every organization's documents, findings, and audit history are isolated
from every other organization's — no organization can see another's data.

## Children's privacy

THIBITISHA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the "last
updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you
accept the updated policy.
