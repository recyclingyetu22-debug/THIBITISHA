## Full description (Play: 4000 char max / App Store: 4000 char max)

**Verify before you trust.**

THIBITISHA examines documents — certificates, invoices, IDs, and more —
and shows you the forensic evidence, in plain language. Not a guess, not
a "verified ✓" badge with no explanation — actual evidence, laid out so
you can judge it yourself.

**What THIBITISHA looks for:**

• **Forensic Assessment** — our detectors examine the document itself and
  report what they find. We never claim a document is "authentic" — we
  show you the evidence and let you decide.

• **Reference Comparison** — when you have a known original to compare
  against, THIBITISHA checks the submitted document directly against it.

• **Structural & typography analysis** — inconsistent fonts, unusual
  edits, and software fingerprints left behind by editing tools.

• **Suspicious region detection** — signatures, seals, photos, and other
  sensitive areas are checked for signs of tampering.

• **AI-generation indicators** — signals that part of a document may have
  been created or altered using AI tools.

**Honest by design.** THIBITISHA never overclaims. Every result separates
what the software found from what a human reviewer decided — clearly,
every time.

**Try it free.** Every new account gets 2 free verifications to see
exactly how it works, no commitment required. After that, verification
packages are available right in the app.

**Who it's for:**

• Individuals who want a quick, honest read on a single document.
• Businesses, banks, universities, and legal teams who need a shared
  investigator workspace with a full audit trail.
